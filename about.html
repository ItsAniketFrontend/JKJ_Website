Our Story

Heritage of Royal, Magnificent
and Purest Jewelry

By JKJ Jewellers, Jaipur — Since 1868

A Legacy of Over 157 Years

Trust, Quality & Tradition,
Eight Generations Strong

JKJ is a symbol of trust of quality in traditional Indian Jewelry with a touch of modernity. This eight-generation-old legacy started in 1868 in the small town of Ratangarh. In 1983, we began introducing and exporting rich traditional Rajasthani Jewelry to UAE and other countries. To serve the people of Jaipur, the very first JKJ store was opened in 1996 at M I Road by Satyanarayan Ji Mosun.

Our Journey

A Legacy Forged
in Gold

From a humble Rajasthan town to international export markets and India's iconic showrooms — the JKJ story is one of resilience, refinement, and devotion to craft. Eight generations of the Mosun family have carried this torch, each adding their own chapter to a legacy written entirely in gold.

Today
5 Showrooms & Growing

Four Jaipur destinations — M I Road, Mansarovar, Vidyadhar Nagar, Jagatpura — and a showroom in Delhi.

World Gold Council Recognition

First to Introduce 22/22 Hallmark Jewellery in India

JKJ Jewellers holds the distinction of being the first jeweller in India to introduce 22/22 Hallmark jewellery — a landmark in certified purity. This pioneering achievement was recognised and awarded by the World Gold Council, cementing JKJ's position at the forefront of quality and transparency in the Indian jewellery industry.
