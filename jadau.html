Designs of Jadau Jewellery &
Our Best Jadau Jewellery in Jaipur

Jadau jewellery in Jaipur is a traditional Indian craft known for its intricate designs and the use of precious gemstones. It features a Kundan setting, where gemstones are meticulously embedded in pure gold. The jewellery often includes colorful Minakari enamel work, creating ornate and regal pieces, typically worn during special occasions.

Since 1868, JKJ Jewellers in Jaipur, Rajasthan, has been a symbol of excellence in jewelry craftsmanship. Founded by Satyanarayan Mosun and now led by Mr. Jatin Mosun, they've built a legacy spanning over a century. Known for their exquisite jadau jewellery in Jaipur, JKJ Jewellers is the oldest and best jewellers in Jaipur, shining as brightly as the gemstones they work with, embodying traditional craftsmanship and creativity.
